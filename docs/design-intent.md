# Design Intent

## 1. Game title and genre

REFRACT - Tower Defense & Strategy.

## 2. Target player and pitch

For players who like short, tactical mobile sessions and spatial puzzles, and want a five-minute run where every encounter is a small problem with a visible answer. REFRACT is a tower defense with no towers. The crystal you defend fires one beam, and you bend, split and bounce it with mirrors and glass so it burns the shadows walking the road instead of merely crossing it.

## 3. How to play (controls)

Tap PLAY. The beam is already firing. Tap a tile in its path to place a mirror and the beam sweeps into its new route. Tap a placed piece to flip, move or sell it, or drag it elsewhere. The bottom buttons choose Mirror, Splitter, Reflector and Lamp; CORE upgrades the beam. Nothing starts until you tap START WAVE, so planning is untimed and rearranging or selling is free. Pause, speed, help and sound sit at the top. Everything is tap and drag, in portrait.

## 4. Core loop

Read the formation named above the board, route the beam along road segments in the right direction, start the encounter, watch it burn, spend the gold it pays, repeat. Feedback is on the board: the beam thins and reddens past every shadow, a shield flares when it turns light away, and core HP counts what got through. Beat all eight encounters to win; lose when the core is spent, or when the boss reaches it. Each formation changes which route is best.

## 5. What is in this prototype

One map; four piece types; a six-level core; six threats including a boss; eight scripted encounters plus endless mode; in-play hints and a help screen; score and best score; synthesized audio; one self-contained offline build. Not included: extra maps, meta-progression, difficulty settings.

## 6. Progression and signature twist

Pieces unlock at encounters 3, 4 and 5, each just before the formation that wants it. Core levels thicken every beam, including lamps. The network spreads across the board as you build, and the roster escalates from motes to runners, shielded Bulwarks, swarms and finally Umbra, which advances behind its shield and drops it halfway down the road.

The signature twist is that light has a direction and so do the things it hits. Every shadow takes a bite out of the beam, so the ones behind take less. A Bulwark also carries its shield on the face it walks towards, and light meeting that face is mostly turned away while light reaching its flank or its back lands in full. The same three mirrors are right or wrong depending on which way everyone is facing.

## 7. Future-state vision

A full game of hand-built maps that each teach a new optical piece - prisms, lenses, coloured light and colour-keyed shadows - with daily seeded challenges and a score chase, built natively on the creation tools.
