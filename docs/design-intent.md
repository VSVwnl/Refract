# Design Intent

## 1. Game title and genre

REFRACT - Tower Defense & Strategy.

## 2. Target player and pitch

For players who like short, tactical mobile sessions and light spatial puzzles, and who want a seven-minute run where every wave is a small problem with a visible answer. REFRACT is a tower defense with no towers. The crystal you defend fires one beam of light, and you bend, split and bounce it with mirrors and glass so it burns the shadows walking the road instead of merely crossing their path.

## 3. How to play (controls)

Tap PLAY. The beam is already firing. Tap a tile in its path to place a mirror and the beam sweeps into its new route. Tap a placed piece to flip, move or sell it, or drag it elsewhere. The buttons along the bottom choose Mirror, Splitter, Reflector and Lamp; CORE upgrades the beam; NEXT WAVE starts early for bonus gold. Pause, double speed, help and sound sit at the top. Everything is tap and drag, in portrait, with no hover.

## 4. Core loop

Read the incoming wave, route the beam so it runs along road segments in the right direction, watch the wave burn, spend the gold it pays, repeat. The feedback is all on the board: the beam visibly thins and reddens past every shadow it passes through, LIT n/31 counts how much road is burning, and core HP counts what got through. Clear twelve waves to win; lose when the core's HP is gone; Endless continues afterwards for score. Each wave's composition changes which route is best, so the same board keeps asking a new question.

## 5. What is in this prototype

One map; four piece types; a six-level core; six enemy types including two bosses; twelve scripted waves plus an endless mode; in-play hints and a help screen; score and best score; synthesized audio; and a single self-contained offline build. Not included: extra maps, meta-progression between runs, difficulty settings.

## 6. Progression and signature twist

Pieces unlock at waves 2, 4 and 7. Core levels thicken and brighten every beam, including the lamps. The light network physically spreads across the board as you build, and the enemy roster escalates from motes to runners, brutes, swarms, a Brute King and finally Umbra.

The signature twist is absorption. Every shadow the light passes through takes a bite out of it, so the ones behind take less. That makes the beam's direction relative to the walk a real decision: against the flow it burns the leader and shields the pack; with the flow it burns the pack and leaves the leader. The same three mirrors are right or wrong depending on who is in front.

## 7. Future-state vision

A full game of hand-built maps that each teach a new optical piece - prisms, lenses, coloured light and colour-keyed shadows - with daily seeded challenges and a score chase, rebuilt natively on the creation tools.
